In **Rooftops in Bloom**, one sprinkler can turn a whole cluster of rooftops into a garden. Find placements that water every rooftop before your supply runs out.

Play five hand-crafted neighborhoods in the campaign, or choose **Endless** for a continuing series of generated rooftop puzzles. From the seventh Endless garden onward, locked rooftops need water from a neighboring sprinkler. Your campaign and Endless games save separately, so you can switch between them.

Download **Rooftops-in-Bloom-Windows-x64.exe** and double-click it to play in English on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. The portable game runs offline and remembers your progress on the computer.
