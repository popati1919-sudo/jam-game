How to play

From roof to roof

✿✿✦✿✿

Make every roof bloom before you run out of sprinklers. A sprinkler waters its own roof and any roof one space north, south, east or west.

Select a roof’s letter to preview the roofs it will water. Choose Place sprinkler or select the same roof again to place it there. You can place a sprinkler on a roof that’s already in bloom.

Endless mode grows a new garden each time you finish one. Later gardens have locked roofs. Place a sprinkler next door to make them bloom.

To play with a keyboard, focus the map. Choose a roof with the arrow keys or its letter. Press Enter to place a sprinkler.
